Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

Disconnect ("we", "our", "the App") is designed to minimize the data we collect. We believe in privacy by design — we only collect what's necessary to make the App work.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not track your app usage for advertising purposes
  • We do not build behavioral profiles or use recommendation algorithms
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third-party advertisers
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs, browsing history, or data from other apps
  • We do not use cookies or tracking pixels

3. Screen Time Tracking

Disconnect tracks your in-app screen time locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your phone and is not sent to our servers. We designed it this way on purpose — your screen time is your business.

4. Location Data

We use your location to show nearby events and beacons. Location is:

  • Approximated to city-block level — never stored as exact coordinates
  • Only accessed when the app is in the foreground
  • Never shared with other users (they see distance, not your position)
  • Never sold or shared with third parties

5. Ephemeral Data

Disconnect is designed around temporary data:

  • Beacons expire and are deleted after 2 hours
  • Event chats are deleted 24 hours after the event ends
  • Circle feeds only show the last 24 hours of activity

This is a core design principle, not a limitation. We believe less stored data = better privacy.

6. Minor Users (13-17)

Users under 18 receive additional protections:

  • Cannot create events, beacons, or circles
  • Can only join events marked "All Ages"
  • Profile is not visible outside events they've joined
  • No direct messaging with strangers

Users under 13 are blocked at registration in compliance with COPPA.

8. Data Retention

Your data is retained until you delete your account. When you delete your account:

  • All personal data is permanently deleted
  • Events you created are deleted
  • All habits, check-ins, and focus sessions are deleted
  • Circle memberships are removed
  • This process is irreversible

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (via the Profile and Settings screens)
  • Delete your account and all data (Settings → Delete Account)
  • Correct your profile information (Edit Profile)
  • Object to data processing by deleting your account

10. Security

All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. Passwords and phone numbers are hashed. We follow industry-standard security practices.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will notify users of material changes via the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact us at privacy@disconnect.app.
