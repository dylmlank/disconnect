## Description

Disconnect is the app designed to get you off your phone and into the world.

Unlike every other social app, Disconnect is built to be used as little as possible. The best session is under 2 minutes. There are no feeds, no infinite scroll, and no algorithm trying to keep you hooked.

**What you can do:**

🌿 **Track Research-Backed Habits**
Choose from 10 habits backed by peer-reviewed research — exercise, nature time, mindfulness, deep work, gratitude journaling, and more. Each habit shows the science behind it. Log with a single tap.

🏆 **Join Challenges**
Take on 7-30 day challenges like Phone-Free Mornings, 30-Day Daily Walk, or Deep Work Week. Join with friends or your city. Daily accountability, no guilt.

🎯 **Focus Sessions**
Start a timed focus session and see who in your circle is working alongside you. No video, no chat — just presence and a timer. Context switching costs 23 minutes. We help you reclaim that.

👥 **Circles**
Create small accountability groups (3-8 people). See each other's habits, check-ins, and progress. Research shows group accountability increases follow-through by 65%.

📝 **Daily Check-ins**
Take 60 seconds to reflect: mood, three good things, and an intention. Based on Seligman's research that just one week of gratitude journaling produces lasting happiness gains.

📅 **Local Events & Beacons**
Discover real-world meetups nearby — coffee, sports, study sessions, volunteering. Or drop a beacon: "I'm at the park, come hang." Expires in 2 hours.

**What makes Disconnect different:**
- No infinite scroll. Anywhere.
- No algorithmic recommendations.
- Nudges you to close the app after 5 minutes.
- Tracks your in-app time as a LOW score.
- Every feature cites the research behind it.
- Ephemeral by design — chats auto-delete, beacons expire, feeds show only 24 hours.

Built for Gen Z and anyone who wants to spend less time scrolling and more time living.
